It is the first quartile at TU/e — and the legendary course Logic & Sets has returned.
Every year, countless students face its challenge… and many barely survive the final exam.

At the center of it all stands Professor Baas Luutik — brilliant, strict, and feared by everyone who forgot to study quantifiers.

But this year, something unexpected happened.

A group of stressed-out students, terrified of the upcoming test, kidnapped Professor Baas and locked him deep inside an underground dungeon hidden beneath MetaForum.
Their plan: No professor = No final exam.

Now the future of logic is in danger.

You are the last hope.
To free him, you must complete the final challenge:
Rebuild the missing proof fragments scattered across the map.
Only a perfectly constructed proof will unlock the dungeon door…

Prepare your brain.
Sharpen your reasoning.
Every symbol matters.

Prove your way to victory — and save Professor Baas!
